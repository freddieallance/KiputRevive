# KiputRevive: Preserving Sarawak’s Linguistic Heritage

## 🚀 Project Overview
**KiputRevive** is an interactive, child-centered educational platform designed to preserve and revive the endangered Kiput language of the Orang Ulu community in Sarawak. This project addresses **Case Study 2: Indigenous Language Preservation** by bridging the digital divide through authentic oral tradition archiving and gamified learning.

## 📖 Brief Summary
Kiput is a low-resource language with a diminishing number of native speakers. **KiputRevive** provides a solution for children aged 5-10 to engage with their mother tongue through:
1. **Multimedia Archives:** Digital e-books featuring authentic oral folktales recorded by a native speaker.
2. **Bilingual Education:** A "bridge" system using English and Bahasa Melayu to teach Kiput vocabulary.
3. **AI Integration:** A conceptual AI "Cultural Guardian" chatbot designed to provide interactive, safe cultural mentorship.

Our methodology focused on **Human-Centered Design**, ensuring that 100% of the linguistic data is sourced directly from the Kiput community rather than unreliable AI translations.

## 📜 AI Disclosure
This project utilizes Generative AI for multimedia asset generation (illustrations) and design methodology brainstorming. **No AI was used to generate Kiput linguistic data.** All Kiput text and audio recordings were provided by a verified native speaker to ensure 100% cultural accuracy and preservation.
